Enhanced FMCG SOP Dataset - Graph Visualization

Dataset Overview

This visualization shows the relationships between products, categories, and countries in the Enhanced FMCG SOP dataset.

  • Blue nodes: Products (SKUs)
  • Green nodes: Categories (Legumes, Dried Fruits, Nuts, Grains, Spices)
  • Red nodes: Countries (Country A, B, C)
  • Edges: Relationships between entities
